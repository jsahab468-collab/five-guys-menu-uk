Burgers

Every burger is made to order with your choice of 15 free toppings, from grilled mushrooms and jalapeños to BBQ sauce and relish. UK burger prices generally run from £9.25 for a Little Hamburger to £14.95 for a Bacon Cheeseburger, with calories ranging from about 464 to 904 kcal depending on size and toppings.

Hot Dogs, Sandwiches & Fries

Hot dogs start at £8.75, sandwiches from £6.45, and the famous boardwalk-style fries come in three sizes: Regular £6.45, Large £7.35, with a smaller Little Fries option at £4.95. Shakes are £6.95 (Little Shake £4.75) with 12 free mix-ins. For the full list of every item, price and calorie count, visit the Five Guys menu with prices UK guide.

Why Check Prices Before You Go?

  • Budgeting: a typical meal — Cheeseburger, Regular Fries and a drink — comes to around £23.55.
  • Calories: that same meal is about 2,094 kcal, so knowing the numbers helps you choose.
  • Toppings are free: all 15 toppings are included, so customising costs nothing extra.

Good to Know

Five Guys first opened in Arlington, Virginia in 1986 and arrived in the UK at Covent Garden, London in 2013. Kids meals are around £8.50. This page is an independent, unofficial guide and is not affiliated with Five Guys.
